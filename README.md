## The entry

> My father pressed his last twenty into my hand the morning I left for the city and said, "Spend it on food, not pride — hunger passes, but a debt to a stranger follows you home."

A real moment, a real person, advice that stays true long after that
morning: pride is the expensive habit, hunger is temporary, and a debt
to a stranger follows you home.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only — the grader installs nothing)
holds the sentence as a constant, validates the gate (UTF-8, 1–200
characters, exactly one sentence, not only whitespace), then writes it
to `/output/entry.txt` (or `./entry.txt` when `/output` does not exist)
and prints its sha256. Deterministic: two runs are byte-identical.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
